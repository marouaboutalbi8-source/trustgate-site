The first big decision

Mainland, Free Zoneor QFC?

It determines ownership, permitted activities and how visas work. We help you make the right choice before anything is filed.

IMainland

Ministry of Commerce and Industry

Mainland

For companies that trade, contract and serve customers anywhere in Qatar.

Ownership
Up to 100% foreign ownership in most sectors, with approval under Law No. 1 of 2019.
Suited to
Trading, contracting, professional services, retail and hospitality.
Legal forms
Limited liability company, branch of a foreign company, single-person company.
IIQatar Free Zones

Qatar Free Zones Authority

Qatar Free Zones

Purpose-built zones at Ras Bufontas and Umm Alhoul for technology, logistics and industry.

Ownership
100% foreign ownership.
Suited to
Technology, logistics, light manufacturing and regional headquarters.
Location
Ras Bufontas, near Hamad International Airport, and Umm Alhoul, near Hamad Port.

QFC Authority

Qatar Financial Centre

Ownership

Suited to
Financial services, consultancy, holding companies and family offices.
Legal system
English common law, with its own regulator and courts.

After the setup

Every renewal,on schedule.

Every company in Qatar manages a series of expiry dates: the CR, the trade licence, the establishment card and every employee's Qatar ID. A lapsed establishment card can halt visa processing for the entire company.

We maintain a single renewal calendar for your company and begin every renewal well ahead of its deadline.

How it goes

Four steps,and you'll knowwhere we are.

  1. 01

    Approve

    We secure the work permit or recruitment approval for the role.

  2. 02

    Issue

    The entry visa is issued and we brief the employee on arrival steps.

  3. 03

    Complete

    We book the medical and fingerprints and follow the results.

  4. 04

    Reside

    The residence permit and Qatar ID are issued, and the renewal date goes into your calendar.

Good to know

Health insurance is now required for expatriate residents, and certificates from abroad often need attestation before a visa can be issued. We tell you early, so arrivals are not delayed.

What to have ready

  • Passport copy and photograph
  • Attested educational certificate, where the role requires it
  • Employment contract details
  • Valid health insurance for the applicant

We'll confirm the exact list for your case before anything is submitted.

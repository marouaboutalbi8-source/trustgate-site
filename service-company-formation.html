What we manage for you

0 of 7

  1. Advice on legal form: LLC, branch, representative office or single-person company
  2. Trade name reservation and activity selection on the MOCI Single Window
  3. Articles of Association drafted, translated and notarized at the Ministry of Justice
  4. Commercial Registration and Qatar Chamber membership
  5. Commercial licence and premises approval with the Ministry of Municipality
  6. Establishment card, labor file and tax registration
  7. Free Zone and Qatar Financial Centre applications

How it works

Four clear steps,with full visibilitythroughout.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We confirm structure, activities, capital and shareholders in one working session.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    We draft the documents, arrange translations and tell you what to sign and where.

  3. 03

    Submit

    We file with each authority in the right order and follow every application in person.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    You receive your CR, licence and cards, with a calendar of every renewal date.

Good to know

Since Law No. 1 of 2019, foreign investors can own up to 100% of a mainland company in most sectors, subject to approval. Some activities still carry conditions, and we check yours before you commit.

What to have ready

  • Passport copies of shareholders and the manager
  • For corporate shareholders: certificate of incorporation, Articles and board resolution, legalized
  • Proposed trade names (three options are useful)
  • A lease or premises plan for the commercial licence

We confirm the exact requirements for your case before anything is submitted.
